[STIMULUS] Over the next months of the 1982 summer, more cases of KS and PCP with an unidentifiable cause of immunosuppression were seen in hemophiliacs, female sex partners of homosexual men, intravenous (injection) drug users who shared needles, and infants. The public, who previously dismissed the disease as a 'gay plague' now has become terrified. The disease officially adopts the name AIDS, and through patterns of infection it is now clear that AIDS is caused by some form of infectious agent. In fear and due to hateful beliefs people had (and have) about gay men and injection drug users, and because the full scope of how the disease could be spread had not been elucidated (people thought that touching could spread disease), a massive stigma (prejudice) developed against people who had or were at risk for AIDS. Furthermore, Haitian immigrants with no known AIDS risk factors had a high prevalence of AIDS. Four high risk groups for AIDS were labeled as the four Hs (homosexual men, heroin addicts, hemophiliacs, and Haitians). The resulting stigma against Haitians would cause diplomacy problems a decade later. Note that in 1983, the virus responsible for AIDS was isolated, and a test was rapidly developed. The test showed that usually those infected with the AIDS virus took around 8 years to progress to AIDS, and that by the time of the first case reports of AIDS, around 200,000 gay men had already been infected.
