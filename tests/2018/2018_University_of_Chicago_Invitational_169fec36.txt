9a. [matching, 7 pt] Match the following to their definitions.
    - Secondary attack rate  <->  a. The proportion of persons with a particular disease or attribute at any time during the interval
    - Period prevalence  <->  b. The attack rate in the exposed group divided by the attack rate in the unexposed group
    - Overall attack rate  <->  c. The number of persons who ate a given food and became ill divided by the total number of persons who ate that food
    - Relative risk  <->  d. The proportion of persons with a particular disease or attribute on a particular date
    - Food-specific attack rate  <->  e. the likelihood that an outcome will occur given a particular exposure, compared to the likelihood of the outcome occurring in the absence of that exposure
    - Point prevalence  <->  f. The total number of new cases divided by the total population
    - Odds ratio  <->  g. Calculated to document the difference between community transmission of illness versus transmission of illness in a household, barracks, or other closed population
