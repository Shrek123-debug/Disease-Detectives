=== Section 5: Name type of bias best displayed by each of the following descriptions. ===

P5-1. [frq, 2 pt] A situation in which the effect or association between an exposure and outcome is distorted by the presence of another variable from the study.

P5-2. [frq, 2 pt] Controls selected for a case control study are more likely to be exposed to pathogen than study population from which they arose.

P5-3. [frq, 2 pt] Error in the selection, division, or sampling of the study population.

P5-4. [frq, 2 pt] A form of selection bias that causes hospital cases and controls in a case control study to be systematically different from one another because the combination of exposure to risk and occurrence of disease increases the likelihood of being admitted to the hospital.

P5-5. [frq, 2 pt] In the clinical research trial industry, lost to follow-up refers to patients who at one point in time were actively participating in a clinical research trial, but have become lost (either by error in a computer tracking system or by being unreachable) at the point of follow-up in the trial.

P5-6. [frq, 2 pt] Error due to the inaccurate categorization of the study population into demographic groups.

P5-7. [frq, 2 pt] Researcher conveys high expectations to subjects, subjects then produce those results.

P5-8. [frq, 2 pt] People act different when they're aware they're being observed.

You learn that 5 of the 60 players on the team have been diagnosed by their doctors with MRSA (methicillin resistant staphylococcus aureus) within the last month. The coach has also informed you that the players have constantly shared towels & washcloths lately & have unfortunately not consistently showered before using the hot tub. It is your job to investigate further!
