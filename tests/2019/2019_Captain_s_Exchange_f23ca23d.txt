[STIMULUS] **Case Study 1**

The city of Fremont, California has a population of 214,089. Before January 2015, there were 467 new cases of whooping cough (pertussis). An outbreak occurred in March at an elementary school in the west side of the city. After June 2015, there were 1209 more cases of whooping cough, with 19 confirmed deaths.

Symptoms of whooping cough include severe/chronic coughing, apnea, fever, congestion, and sneezing.
