=== Section D: A Puddle of Trouble ===

[STIMULUS] In the rural county of Willow Creek, public health officials noticed an unusual increase in reported cases of Legionnaires' disease during the months of July and August 2023. Typically, the county sees only a handful of cases per year, but during this period, 35 cases were reported. Legionnaires' disease, a severe form of pneumonia caused by Legionella bacteria, is often associated with contaminated water sources.

Recognizing the potential for a localized outbreak, the Willow Creek Health Department initiated an investigation. A "case" was defined as any resident of Willow Creek County with laboratory-confirmed Legionnaires' disease diagnosed between July 1 and August 31, 2023. Through hospital records and physician reporting, 35 cases were identified. To identify potential risk factors, a case-control study was designed. Two controls were selected for each case, totaling 70 controls.

Preliminary data analysis indicated that a significantly higher proportion of cases reported spending time in a newly opened community hot tub facility compared to controls. Further testing of the hot tub water showed high levels of Legionella bacteria. 20 of the cases had reported spending time in the new hot tub facility and 30 of the controls had reported spending time in the new hot tub facility.
