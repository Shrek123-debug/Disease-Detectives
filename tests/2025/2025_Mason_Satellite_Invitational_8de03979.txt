Though the majority of listeria infections are mild, in severe cases, it can result in blood infection or meningitis (inflammation of the brain or spinal cord), which can be fatal [2]. Individuals who are especially at risk include pregnant women, infants and small children, and those who are immunocompromised (65+ years old, underlying health conditions, and so forth) [2].
