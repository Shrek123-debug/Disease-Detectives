=== Part II: Malaria ===

[STIMULUS] Malaria is a serious, life-threatening disease endemic to tropical areas of the world, including sub-Saharan Africa, South America, and Southeast Asia. It is caused by a protozoan parasite, Plasmodium falciparum, and spread by mosquitoes. Once in the bloodstream, P. falciparum travels to the liver where it matures and reproduces in a dormant state. After a period of time, the parasite can then travel back into the bloodstream where it infects and kills red blood cells. This results in a time of onset of around 10-15 days. Symptoms of malaria include chills, fever (often in waves), sweating, fatigue, and pain in the abdomen or muscles. Annually, there are around 263 million cases of malaria globally with a prevalence of 60 cases per 1000 individuals. The mortality rate of malaria hovers around 14 deaths per 100,000 individuals with around 600 thousand deaths per year. There is no vaccine for malaria, however antimalarial treatments do exist. If treated properly and early enough, malaria can be cured.
