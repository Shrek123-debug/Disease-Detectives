=== SECTION #3: Avian Influenza ===

[STIMULUS] H5N1, more commonly known as avian influenza or "bird flu", is a strain of Influenza A that most commonly infects poultry. In recent years, H5N1, which was mainly confined to Asia, has spread globally and into new species of mammals, endangering wildlife, agricultural production— and potentially—human health. Indeed, in rare cases, humans can get sick from infected animals.

Although person-to-person transmission has not been observed, public health officials are deeply concerned about the devastating potential of a variant known as Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI), which has been documented to have a mortality rate of nearly 50%. As such, it is a key public health priority to prevent an H5N1 pandemic before it manifests.

=== SECTION #4: Alzheimer's Disease ===

[STIMULUS] Alzheimer's disease is a disease that impairs memory and other essential functions, which often has a delayed diagnosis due to its progressive nature. In late-stages, the individual may lose the ability to walk or stand without assistance. No conclusive evidence determines whether infectious agents are responsible for AD.
