[STIMULUS] **Scenario 1: Foodborne Listeriosis**

The bacteria is most commonly spread through raw or undercooked food, although vertical mother-to-fetus transmission is also possible. Horizontal transmission does not occur. Listeriosis has a typical incubation period of <24 hours and a duration of 1-5 days.

[STIMULUS] **Scenario 2: Trichodysplasia Spinulosa**

You are a researcher studying trichodysplasia spinulosa (TS), an extremely rare skin condition that appears almost exclusively in immunocompromised patients (usually organ transplant recipients on regimens of immunosuppressive drugs). TSPyV is capable of person-to-person transmission, primarily from mother to child and between siblings. Although the precise route of transmission is unknown, there is evidence that the upper respiratory tract may be an important site of infection.
