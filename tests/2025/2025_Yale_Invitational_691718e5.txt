=== Section 2: Investigation 1 ===

[STIMULUS] Hepatitis A is a virus that is transmitted either with direct contact or ingestion of food or water that has been contaminated with the virus, through the fecal-oral route.

On November 1, 2003, the Pennsylvania Department of Health was alerted by Restaurant A, reporting from Beaver County, PA, about an ongoing illness. The year prior, only one such case had been reported. That day, the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture underwent an investigation and found no sanitary violations had occurred, but learned the case count was much higher: 13 employees had become ill after October 26. By the 7th, 111 cases had been reported.

Knowing that Hepatitis A has an incubation period of 15 to 50 days, an investigation was prompted by looking at records starting from October 3rd. In Pennsylvania, cases were identified through electronic disease-surveillance systems, and an interview was attempted with every reported case, and any other residents who had eaten at Restaurant A. Suspected samples were taken into diagnostic laboratories to be confirmed via RNA sequence analysis, where a 315-nucleotide segment of VP1-P2A junction was amplified and compared to sequence surveillance databases at CDC. Those confirmed had serologic matching of acute infection with IgM antibody to hepatitis A virus.

At the end of the outbreak on December 1st, out of the more than 9,300 restaurant goers, 601 patients had been identified. Three died; at least 124 were hospitalized. Of 425 patients who recalled a single dining date at the restaurant, 356 (84 percent) had dined there between October 3 and October 6.

=== Section 3: Investigation 2 - COVID-19 in the Middle East ===

[STIMULUS] During the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic, Iran was one of the first countries outside China to be affected in a major way. By July 2021, the country was being impacted by a fourth wave of the disease. During this wave, scientists conducted a study with the aim of investigating the epidemiological characteristics and mapping the spatiotemporal trend of SARS-CoV-2 in the important city of Mashhad in Iran.

Data was collected by the Mashhad University of Medical sciences, covering the 3 months from February 14, 2020 to May 11, 2020. Suspected cases were referred to healthcare centers and were confirmed either clinically or by laboratory tests. For unequivocal confirmation of infection, a Reverse Transcription PCR test was required. Information collected on confirmed cases included patients' residence address, sex, age, date of symptoms onset, Length of Stay (LOS) in the hospital, underlying diseases, common symptoms, exposure history, disease severity and disease outcome.

Continuous variables were expressed as median and Inter Quartile Range (IQR). Categorical variables were expressed as number and percentage and compared by a Chi Squared test. To determine the effect of age, sex, ICU admission, LOS and co-morbidities on mortality, univariate logistic regression was used. Un-adjusted Odds Ratio with a 95% Confidence Interval was also applied. To do this, 2,943 patients with a certified outcome (death or recovery) were included.
