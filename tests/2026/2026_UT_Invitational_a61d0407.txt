|---|---|

| 2. The cause and effect have a 1:1 relationship. | B. Coherence |
| 3. An increasing amount of exposure to the agent increases disease risk. | C. Analogy |
| 4. Exposure always precedes effect. | D. Consistency |
| 5. Similar relationships have been observed in other contexts. | E. Specificity |
| 6. The relationship is consistent with biological principles. | F. Plausibility |
| 7. The condition can be prevented or induced by controlling conditions. | G. Experiment |
| 8. The relationship is compatible with current theory and practice. | H. Biological Gradient |
| 9. There is high correlation between variables. | J. Temporality |
