=== It's That Shrimple ===

[STIMULUS] In a coastal Southeast Asian community with widespread fishing and aquaculture, a sudden outbreak of severe gastroenteritis has affected 83 individuals over the past 6 weeks. Stool cultures have identified Vibrio parahaemolyticus (Vp) in most cases. Several Vp isolates were resistant to tetracycline, sulfonamides, and fluoroquinolones. The area has multiple shrimp farms known to have poor sanitation and excessive antibiotic use to protect shrimp from disease. Epidemiologists decided to test a modified version of the SEIR model that incorporates environmental Vp concentrations.

**Background:**
The SEIR model divides the population into four categories, called "S", "E", "I", and "R".
- Category "S" consists of individuals who are susceptible to the disease being modeled.
- Category "E" consists of individuals who are exposed to the disease. Diseases (like COVID-19) often have an incubation period or a latency period, and this category accommodates it.
- The incubation rate σ is the rate at which exposed hosts become infected
- Category "I" consists of individuals infected with the disease who are capable of infecting others.
- The recovery rate γ is the rate at which infected individuals move to the R category:
- Category "R" consists of individuals who can be removed from the system, e.g., because they have gained immunity to the disease, or because they have succumbed to the disease.
- β is the average number of susceptible individuals infected by one infectious individual per contact per unit of time
- N is the total population; N = S + E + I + R.

The following set of partial ordinary differential equations models the SEIR system. Use this as a reference for this section:

dS/dt = − βI/N S,
dE/dt = βI/N S − σE,
dI/dt = σE − γI,
dR/dt = γI
