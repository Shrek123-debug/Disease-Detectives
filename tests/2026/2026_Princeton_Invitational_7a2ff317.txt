[STIMULUS] We can model the spread of KoRV through a Koala population using a compartmental model. This model is defined by the following system of differential equations. The notation d𝑥/d𝑡 denotes the change in 𝑥 per change in 𝑡, which in our case is time.

d𝑆/d𝑡 = − ρ · α · 𝑆𝐼/𝑁

d𝐼/d𝑡 = ρ · α · 𝑆𝐼/𝑁 − γ𝐼
