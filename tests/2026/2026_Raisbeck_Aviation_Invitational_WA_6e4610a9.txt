=== Part ii. Outbreak Investigation ===

[STIMULUS] Kanishka Cuisine of India in Seattle offered a “Fusion Thanksgiving Feast” for pickup by customers or delivery for 11/27/2025. (This was a real case). 43 people reported being sick after eating this meal between 11/27 and 11/28. These people reported having diarrhea, stomach pains, vomiting, nausea, headaches, and fatigue.

The King County Public Health interviewed 21 people to see what they had eaten. King County was unable to link this illness to one particular food item. Four people were tested for norovirus, Shiga toxin-producing E. coli, and Salmonella. These tests came back negative, but the tests didn’t check for bacteria that cause toxins. Bacteria that create toxins tend to have a quick onset and by the time a patient gets to the doctor it is too late to test.

King County conducted an on-site inspection on December 2 and found the following issues:

- The cooling methods most likely didn’t bring the food down to a safe temperature.
- Some foods weren’t kept cold enough to prevent bacterial toxin growth.
- There was inadequate equipment and space for the preparation of the “Feast” and their regular food service.

Additional employee training was provided.

King County revisited on December 4 and found the same issues with cooling and keeping foods at room temperature that were supposed to be refrigerated. They closed the restaurant and threw away unsafe foods. Retraining was held.

After an inspection on December 10, Kanishka was allowed to reopen.
