=== Part I: Flying Spaghetti Restaurant Investigation ===

[STIMULUS] The manager of the Flying Spaghetti Monster restaurant reported receiving 18 complaints of gastrointestinal illness, characterized by diarrhea and vomiting, from patrons who ate at the restaurant on November 23, 2019. The State Department of Health started an investigation to confirm the outbreak, assess exposures and recommend measures to prevent continued spread. Samples from the affected patrons tested positive for norovirus using PCR. The manager provided contact information, seating times and seating location for all patrons with reservations. All patrons with contact information were interviewed, and a questionnaire was used to assess illness and exposures. The manager mentioned that there was a sick patron who vomited that day in a private dining area in the restaurant, and the vomitus was cleaned up by staff who later served pecan pie.

1. [mcq, 1 pt] What would be the right term to describe this event?
    A) Outbreak
    B) Endemic
    C) Pandemic
    D) Cluster

2. [frq, 3 pt] Provide a probable case definition for the event detailed above.

[STIMULUS] Interviewing the patrons at that day identified the number of case patients, both confirmed and probable. The interviewers also identified 101 additional individuals who ate at the restaurant, but did not fall ill, and interviewed them about what they ate and where they sat in order to identify the likely source of the outbreak.

3. [mcq, 1 pt] What study design did the investigators use based on the above description?
    A) Randomized control trial
    B) Cohort study
    C) Cross-sectional study
    D) Case-control study
