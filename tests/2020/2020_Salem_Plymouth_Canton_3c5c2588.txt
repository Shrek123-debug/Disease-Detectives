=== Part III: Scenario ===

[STIMULUS] Background II: Outbreak in Texas

The Chief Medical Officer of the Ontario Health Department notified the Texas Department of Health (TDH) about the cyclospora infections in the three Canadian businessmen. The businessmen had attended a meeting at a private club in Houston, Texas on May 9 & 10.

A total of 270 people had attended the Houston business meeting. Participants came from three US states and Canada. Meals served during the meeting were prepared at the restaurant operated by the private club. Rumors among the staff suggested that other attendees at the meeting had also become ill.

TDH, the Houston Health and Human Services Department and the CDC initiated an epidemiologic investigation to identify the source of the outbreak.
