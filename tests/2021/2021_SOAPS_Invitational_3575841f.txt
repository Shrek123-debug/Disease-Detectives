    A) If the susceptible fraction of a population is 1/R₀, then a disease has optimal probability of propagating
    B) The size of the population and the value of R₀ are inversely related
    C) R₀ is directly proportional to the severity of a disease
    D) If the susceptible fraction of a population is > 1/R₀, then only can a disease progress

[STIMULUS] The SIR (susceptible—infected—recovered) model can be adopted to describe COVID-19. Such a model uses some assumptions: the population size is constant, rates (transmission, removal rates) are constant, no demography (births and deaths), well-mixed population.

Let *s* be the number of susceptible individuals, *i* the number of infected individuals, and *r* the number of recovered individuals in a population with a total of *N* individuals. As predicted, an individual in the *s* group moves to the *i* group upon infection and to the *r* group upon recovery.
