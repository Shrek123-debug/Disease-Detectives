Unfortunately, Jen doesn't always remember to wash her hands after going to the bathroom and she was later determined to be an asymptomatic carrier of the Norovirus. Many other students who went through the hamburger line had reported similar symptoms as Lauren. Luckily, Lauren and the other students recovered and were back in school the next day; however, her favorite lunch lady, Jen, was let go from Jonas Salk High School Cafeteria.
