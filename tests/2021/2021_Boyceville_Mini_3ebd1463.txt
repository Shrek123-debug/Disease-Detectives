[STIMULUS] For all culture-confirmed cases, laboratory tests confirmed that all cases were infected with the same bacteria. Sanitary practices revealed some minor violations, where food handling was not properly conducted and one restaurant lacked any soap dispenser in the employee restroom; otherwise, no probable source of the bacteria which caused the outbreak could be found in the local area.
